Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This app deals with something private, so we collect as little as we can. This policy explains what we store, why, and how to delete it. It covers the StopFap app and this website. The data controller is [seller full name], Kazakhstan.

The short version

  • The app does not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t show ads, don’t use advertising trackers and don’t sell or share your data for marketing.
  • Content blocking runs on your phone through Apple’s Screen Time. We never see what you browse.
  • You can delete everything from the app’s settings in one tap.

What we store on our server

When you first open the app, it creates an anonymous account (a random ID) on our database provider, Supabase. Under that ID we store:

DataWhy
Streak dates, best streak, check-insTo keep your progress if you change phones and to show your partner how you’re doing.
Relapse dates, the trigger you chose and any note you addTo build your trigger analytics.
Diary entries, mood and urge scoresTo sync your diary. Only you can read them.
Experience points and avatar rankTo keep your avatar progress.
Nickname, streak and goal you share with a partner or squad; messages you send themTo run the partner and squad features. Only the people you paired with can see them.
Premium status and its end dateTo unlock Premium on your account.

Access rules on the database make sure each account can read only its own data, plus what a partner or squad member chose to share.

When you buy Premium on this website

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your email, billing country, payment details and tax information to process the order, under the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy. We never receive your card number.

Lemon Squeezy sends us only what we need to unlock Premium: the anonymous app ID passed from the app and the subscription status and dates. We don’t store your email or name — for billing and refund questions, Lemon Squeezy looks up your order. When you enter the key from your receipt in the app, the app checks it directly with Lemon Squeezy.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address for security. The site sets no cookies and has no analytics. Fonts are served from this site, not from third parties.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — database hosting for the app.
  • Lemon Squeezy (a Stripe company) — payments for web purchases.
  • Apple — in-app purchases and Screen Time, under Apple’s own policies.
  • GitHub — hosting of this website.

We share data with authorities only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

App data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account in the app, your server data is erased right away. Messages you already sent to a squad stay in that chat, but without your nickname. Order records are kept by Lemon Squeezy and by us for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your rights

You can see, export, correct or delete your data. Most of it you can delete yourself in the app (Settings → Delete account). For anything else — including requests under the CCPA/CPRA if you live in California, or the GDPR if you live in the EU or UK — write to us at [support email]. We will reply within 30 days. We don’t sell or “share” personal information as those laws define it.

Age

The app is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger.
